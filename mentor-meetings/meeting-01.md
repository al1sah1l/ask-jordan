## 2. Project Summary

### Problem / Bad Situation
- George is a middle schooler who wants to discover his interests but doesn't know where to start.
- Sticks to familiar activities (math, soccer) or follows peers.
- Social pressure, limited exposure, and parental influence can prevent students from exploring new interests.

### Proposed Solution — Ask Jordan
- AI-personalized, interactive storytelling app for middle schoolers.
- Students share existing interests and things they're curious about.
- AI generates short stories exploring different activities and fields.
- Students reflect on what they liked and choose what to explore next.
- Goal: encourage exploration without labeling students or prescribing interests.

### Potential Features
- Personalized stories and recommendations.
- Reflection journal.
- Optional real-world activities.
- Social discovery and parental view (stretch goals).

## 3. Issues & Questions

### Problem Framing
- Is our bad situation specific enough?
- Does storytelling actually address the lack of exploration?

### Scope & Implementation
- What's a realistic MVP for three people?
- Which features should we prioritize or cut?
- How should we approach AI integration and personalization?
- What would make this an interesting software design project?

### User Testing
- How can we recruit middle school students for testing?
- Can we test with older students, parents, or teachers instead?

### Ethics & Privacy
- What privacy considerations apply when building for minors?
- How should we handle students' personal reflections and AI-generated content?
